About Our Practice

Dentistry That Respects Your Time & Intelligence

Dr. Sarah Mitchell founded Clearview Dental after a decade at practices where she felt patients weren't getting the attention and transparency they deserved. Her philosophy is simple: treat every patient the way you'd treat a family member.

Located on Congress Avenue in Downtown Austin, our practice was designed from the ground up for the busy professional — efficient appointments, transparent pricing, and a team that actually listens.

We use digital impressions, 3D imaging, and same-day crown technology so that when you leave our office, the work is done.
